Possible directions

Illustrative pathways based on common interests. Not personal recommendations yet.

🔬

Research & discovery

For people energised by questions, evidence, investigation and solving complex problems.

Try: Read one accessible research paper and explain its central question to a friend.
🤝

People & support

For people drawn to listening, teaching, guiding, caring or helping others progress.

Try: Mentor someone for one week and reflect on which parts felt meaningful.
🛠️

Building & creating

For people who enjoy making things, improving systems and turning ideas into useful products.

Try: Build a tiny solution to one everyday problem.
🌍

Community & impact

For people motivated by social problems, collaboration and making a difference at scale.

Try: Volunteer in a local initiative and observe what role suits you.
YOUR FIRST 7 DAYS

Turn reflection into evidence.

A gentle starter plan. Small actions beat grand declarations.

1
Notice

For two days, note activities that give you energy or drain it.

2
Ask

Ask two people who know you well: “When have you seen me at my best?”

3
Explore

Choose one possible direction and speak to someone doing that work.

4
Experiment

Try one small, low-risk activity related to that direction.

5
Reflect

What surprised you? What felt engaging? What would you change?
