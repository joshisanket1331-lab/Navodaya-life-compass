EXPLORE, DON'T LABEL

Possible directions

Use these as doors to investigate, not boxes to fit into.

🔬

Research & discovery

Questions, evidence, investigation and solving complex problems.

Experiment: Read one accessible research paper and explain its central question to a friend.
🤝

People & support

Listening, teaching, guiding, caring and helping others progress.

Experiment: Mentor someone for one week. Notice which parts feel meaningful.
🛠️

Building & creating

Making things, designing, improving systems and turning ideas into useful products.

Experiment: Build a tiny solution to one everyday problem.
🧩

Organising & leading

Coordinating people, making plans, taking responsibility and creating momentum.

Experiment: Organise a small project with a clear goal and deadline.
🌍

Exploration & learning

New ideas, cultures, experiences, adaptability and continuous learning.

Experiment: Try a new skill or visit a place that broadens your perspective.
🌱

Community & impact

Social problems, collaboration and improving life beyond your immediate circle.

Experiment: Volunteer in a local initiative and observe what role suits you.
